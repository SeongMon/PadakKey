IDLE

Preserve the black eye with white glint, black throat bib, crown stripe, two black wing bars, silhouette, position, scale, pixel grid, all other colors exactly. FLAP
Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the exact edit target and idle animation frame. Create the wings-UP frame for the same tiny gray pixel sparrow. Change ONLY the visible folded wing on the right side of its body: lift and extend that wing diagonally upward toward the upper right, keeping its black feather bars and gray fill. Expose pale belly where folded wing was. Keep head, eye, cheek WITHOUT any spot, beak, crown, throat bib, feet, belly silhouette, tail and their exact pixel positions, scale, palette and grid unchanged. No body bounce, no face changes, no new objects. Same 1280 square canvas and transparent background. Single bird, pixel art, no labels. The wing must fit within canvas; bird itself must not be repositioned or resized.
